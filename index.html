01 / EQUIPMENT

FEEDERS & WATERERS

Choosing everyday equipment

Consider flock size, bird age, access and how easily each item can be cleaned. Check dimensions and capacity against your own setup.

Before you set it up
  • Place equipment where birds can reach it and you can inspect it.
  • Keep water clean and fresh, and clean feeders and waterers regularly.
  • Follow the manufacturer's assembly and cleaning instructions.

02 / FEED

FEED & STORAGE

Match feed to your flock

Birds at different life stages have different feed needs. Use the product label to check the intended birds, storage guidance and feeding directions.

Questions to ask
  • Is the feed intended for this type and age of bird?
  • Can it be kept dry, secure and protected from pests?
  • Is fresh, clean water available alongside feed?

03 / HEALTH

MEDICATION & SUPPLEMENTS

Know before you use it

Check that a product is suitable for poultry and your birds' circumstances. A local poultry veterinarian can help identify a health problem and advise on treatment.

What to check on the label
  • Intended species, purpose, directions and storage.
  • Any egg or meat withdrawal instructions, where applicable.
  • When unsure, ask a veterinarian before use. Keep the packaging available for the conversation.
